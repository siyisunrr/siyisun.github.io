Siyi Sun   孙思毅

I am an M.S. student in New Energy Technology at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Safakath Karuthedath. I received my B.Eng. in Materials Science in July 2026 from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications (NJUPT).

At NJUPT, I worked on high-Q and high-CD optical metasurfaces in the Optoelectronic Devices Group. I was also a visiting student at Westlake University, where I worked with Prof. Siying Peng in the Nanophotonics Materials Lab on neuromorphic computing for memristors and phase segregation in mixed-halide perovskites. In the summer of 2025, I attended a summer school at The University of Manchester, where I studied Nobel Prize physics experiments on holography and optical tweezers with Prof. Mark Dickinson.

Research Interests: Optoelectronic Materials, NanoPhotonics & Chip Packaging
